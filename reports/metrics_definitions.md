# Metric Definitions

Consistent metric definitions applied across all SQL queries, Python scripts, and dashboard visualisations.

---

## Core Unit Economics Metrics

**Net Revenue**
Order value for non-refunded orders. Refunded orders have net revenue of zero.
`net_revenue = order_value × (1 - refund_flag)`

**Total Cost**
Sum of all variable costs at the order level.
`total_cost = variable_cost + delivery_cost + support_cost`

**Contribution Margin**
Revenue remaining after all variable costs. This is the primary profitability metric throughout the analysis. Fixed costs are intentionally excluded — the focus is unit-level economics, not total P&L.
`contribution_margin = net_revenue - total_cost`

**Margin %**
Contribution margin as a percentage of net revenue.
`margin_pct = contribution_margin / net_revenue × 100`

---

## Customer-Level Metrics

**Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**
Total marketing spend on a channel divided by the number of customers acquired through that channel. First-touch attribution is used — 100% of spend credited to the signup channel.
`CAC = total_channel_spend / customers_acquired_via_channel`

**Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**
Cumulative contribution margin generated by a customer across all their orders in the observation window (Jan 2024 – Dec 2025).
`LTV = SUM(contribution_margin) per customer`

**LTV:CAC Ratio**
The fundamental unit economics health metric. Values below 1.0x mean the business spends more acquiring customers than it earns from them. Industry benchmark for sustainable on-demand services is ≥ 3.0x.
`ltv_to_cac_ratio = avg_ltv / cac`

**Payback Period**
Number of months until cumulative contribution margin per customer equals or exceeds CAC. Calculated by matching cumulative monthly margin against CAC threshold.

---

## Cohort Metrics

**Cohort**
Group of customers who signed up in the same calendar month. Used as the primary cohort definition throughout.

**Month N Retention**
Percentage of a cohort that placed at least one order exactly N months after their signup month.
`retention_m1 = customers_active_in_month_1 / cohort_size`

**Cumulative LTV**
Sum of contribution margin per cohort member from Month 0 through Month N. Used to track how lifetime value builds (or erodes) over time.

---

## Operational Metrics

**Loss Rate**
Percentage of orders with negative contribution margin.
`loss_rate = orders_with_negative_margin / total_orders`

**Support Ratio**
Support cost as a percentage of order revenue. Key diagnostic metric for identifying high-cost service types and customer segments.
`support_ratio = support_cost / order_value × 100`

**Cost Ratio**
Total cost as a percentage of order value. Values above 100% indicate the order costs more to fulfil than it generates in revenue.
`cost_ratio = total_cost / order_value × 100`

---

## Health Thresholds

| Metric | Healthy | Warning | Critical |
|--------|---------|---------|---------|
| LTV:CAC ratio | ≥ 3.0x | 1.0–3.0x | < 1.0x |
| Margin % | ≥ 10% | 0–10% | < 0% |
| Loss rate | < 15% | 15–30% | > 30% |
| Support ratio | < 12% | 12–20% | > 20% |

---

## Governance Notes

- Contribution margin is used in preference to gross margin throughout. Gross margin typically excludes support and some delivery costs. Contribution margin includes all variable costs that change with each order, giving a more accurate picture of unit-level economics.
- Metrics are calculated identically in SQL, Python analysis scripts, and the Streamlit dashboard. Any discrepancy should be treated as a bug.
- All monetary values are in Indian Rupees (₹). Formatting uses L (lakh = 100,000) and Cr (crore = 10,000,000) for large values.
- Synthetic data is used throughout. Findings are illustrative of real analytical patterns but do not represent any actual company.
