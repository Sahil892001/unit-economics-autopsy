## Scenario Modeling Summary

Several scenarios were evaluated to assess potential paths to improved profitability.

### Key Findings
- A modest 5% price increase produces a meaningful uplift in total contribution margin
- Loss-making orders materially drag overall profitability despite contributing revenue
- Support cost reductions offer high leverage due to heavy-tail cost behavior

### Strategic Implications
- Margin improvement does not require aggressive growth
- Targeted pricing and operational changes outperform blanket cost cutting
- Eliminating or restructuring toxic segments yields immediate benefits
