# Causal Considerations

This analysis identifies strong relationships between acquisition channels, support costs, and contribution margin erosion. However, results are based on observational data and therefore do not imply causality.

## Limitations
- Acquisition channels are not randomly assigned
- Customer behavior may be influenced by unobserved factors
- Support cost intensity may be both a cause and a consequence of customer behavior

## How Causality Could Be Strengthened
- Difference-in-differences analysis around pricing or policy changes
- A/B testing acquisition channels for downstream profitability
- Controlled experiments on support eligibility or service limits

## Interpretation Guidance
Findings should be interpreted as directional signals for decision-making rather than definitive causal proof.
