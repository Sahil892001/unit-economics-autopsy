# Segment Profitability Notes

## Summary

Profitability analysis across three dimensions: service type, region, and acquisition channel. Every region is loss-making. Standard service is the primary margin destroyer. Organic channel customers are the only segment with positive margin per customer.

---

## By Service Type

| Service | Orders | Margin % | Total Margin | Support as % of Revenue |
|---------|--------|----------|--------------|------------------------|
| Express | 47,306 | +7.4% | +₹4.6L | 10.6% |
| Premium | 53,646 | +6.5% | +₹3.1L | 15.8% |
| **Standard** | **98,988** | **-17.7%** | **-₹8.7L** | **27.8%** |

Standard is the highest-volume service (49.5% of orders) and the only loss-making one. Its support cost ratio of 27.8% of revenue is the primary problem — nearly 3× that of Express. Standard orders are also refunded at a higher rate and attract a higher proportion of support-heavy customers.

**Implication:** Express and Premium alone generate +₹7.7L in combined margin. Standard alone destroys ₹8.7L. The business would be profitable if Standard's support cost ratio could be brought below 15%.

## By Region

| Region | Orders | Margin % | Total Margin |
|--------|--------|----------|--------------|
| North | 50,487 | -0.46% | -₹18.5K |
| South | 50,572 | -0.46% | -₹18.7K |
| West | 50,308 | -0.45% | -₹18.5K |
| East | 48,633 | -0.85% | -₹41.4K |

All four regions are loss-making. East underperforms the others by roughly 2× on margin percentage. East has slightly higher delivery and support costs relative to order values, suggesting a combination of lower average order values and higher operational costs in that geography.

Regional differences are relatively small in magnitude compared to the service-type differences — fixing Standard service would benefit all regions.

## By Acquisition Channel

| Channel | Customers | Margin/Customer | Margin % |
|---------|-----------|----------------|---------|
| Organic | 6,078 | +₹5.01 | Positive |
| Social | 1,612 | -₹8.77 | Negative |
| Paid Search | 9,005 | -₹9.67 | Negative |
| Referral | 3,305 | -₹10.60 | Negative |

Organic customers generate positive margin per customer. All other channels are negative. This is consistent with the CAC/LTV analysis — Organic customers self-select for the platform (no paid incentive), tend to order higher-value services, and generate fewer support tickets.

---

## Service × Channel Cross-Tab

The worst combination is **Standard service ordered by Paid Search customers** — combining the lowest-margin service with the lowest-quality acquisition channel. The best combination is **Express service from Organic customers**, which represents the most economically valuable customer segment in the entire business.

---

## Support-Heavy Customer Segment

Top 10% of customers by support cost (approximately 2,000 customers):
- Average support cost: ₹470.58 (vs ₹102 for regular customers)
- Average lifetime margin: **-₹77.81** (vs +₹2.81 for regular customers)
- Only 29.9% are profitable (vs 54% of regular customers)

These 2,000 customers are responsible for a disproportionate share of total losses. Interventions such as tiered support pricing, proactive resolution programs, or service eligibility limits for repeat high-cost tickets could significantly improve overall margins.

---

## Power Users

Top 5% of customers by order volume (1,000 customers):
- Average 47.9 orders per customer (vs 8.0 for regular)
- 24% of total revenue
- Average margin per customer: **-₹26.79** (vs -₹4.12 for regular)

Power users generate volume but worse economics. High order frequency amplifies whatever margin is generated per order — negative in this case. Power user pricing, loyalty tiers, or service restrictions could convert high-frequency customers from margin-negative to margin-positive.
