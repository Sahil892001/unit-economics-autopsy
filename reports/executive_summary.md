# Executive Summary — Unit Economics Intelligence Platform

## Situation

A fast-growing on-demand services platform is generating strong order volume and revenue growth, yet contribution margin has collapsed from **+13.7% in January 2024 to -7.5% by December 2025** — a 21 percentage point deterioration over 24 months. Total contribution margin across the full period is **-₹1.05L on ₹1.59Cr revenue**. The business is structurally loss-making at the unit level.

---

## Root Causes

### 1. Cost Inflation Outpacing Revenue Growth
Delivery cost per order rose approximately **30% over 2 years** due to logistics inflation, erasing margin gains from volume growth. Support costs spike every December by ~35%, making peak season simultaneously the highest-revenue and most loss-making period.

### 2. Standard Service Is Destroying Value
Standard service — the highest-volume product line — operates at **-17.7% contribution margin**, destroying ₹8.7L in value. Its support cost alone consumes **27.8% of revenue**, compared to 10.6% for Express. Express (+7.4%) and Premium (+6.5%) remain margin-positive but cannot offset Standard's losses at current volume mix.

### 3. Paid Search Economics Are Unsustainable
The business spends ₹309 to acquire each Paid Search customer, who generates **-₹9.67 in lifetime contribution margin** — a net loss of ₹319 per customer. Paid Search drives approximately 45% of customer acquisition. Organic is the only channel with positive LTV (₹5.01/customer) at a CAC of ₹71.58. No channel reaches CAC payback within 24 months. Industry benchmark for LTV:CAC is 3.0x; every channel is below 0.1x.

### 4. Acquisition Quality Has Degraded
Recent cohorts (2025) show higher Month-1 retention rates (~90%) than older cohorts (~70%), which appears positive. However, 2024 cohorts generate positive LTV while most 2025 cohorts generate negative LTV. Higher retention of unprofitable customers accelerates losses rather than creating value. This is an acquisition quality problem, not a retention problem.

---

## Recommendations

| Priority | Action | Estimated Margin Impact |
|----------|--------|------------------------|
| 1 | Remove or reprice deep loss-making orders (margin < -₹50) | +₹13.1L |
| 2 | Increase prices by 5% across all service types | +₹7.9L |
| 3 | Reduce support costs by 15% via self-service / tiered support | +₹4.2L |
| 4 | Reallocate 30% of Paid Search budget to Organic | +₹0.4L |
| Combined | Apply all four levers simultaneously | +₹23L+ |

---

## Risk of Inaction

Every month the business continues at current unit economics, it compounds losses. Continued investment in Paid Search acquisition at ₹309 CAC with negative LTV accelerates the problem. Growth is not the solution — fixing the unit economics before scaling is.

---

*Analysis based on 200,000 orders across 20,000 customers, January 2024 – December 2025.*
